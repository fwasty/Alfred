# Slack Command Center — SOP (Template)

## Purpose
Slack is the single place we track leads, missed calls, reviews, and weekly performance.

## Channels
- `#leads` — all new leads + booking updates
- `#missed-calls` — missed calls + callback status
- `#reviews` — review requests + new reviews + reply drafts
- `#ops` — day-to-day questions + changes
- `#weekly-report` — KPI summaries and weekly notes

## Roles
- **Owner/Admin:** Client owner
- **Managers:** office manager/dispatcher
- **Partner:** (You) via Slack Connect or guest access

## Lead handling rules
### When a lead arrives
Post in `#leads`:
- Name (if known)
- Phone
- Service requested
- Zip / service area
- Urgency (today/this week/quote)
- Source (call/web/form)

### When a lead is booked
Reply in-thread:
- “BOOKED” + date/time
- Assigned tech (if relevant)

### When a lead is not qualified
Reply in-thread:
- “NOT A FIT” + reason (outside area / wrong service / spam)

## Missed calls
- Every missed call posts in `#missed-calls`.
- First action: callback OR text-back within 5–10 minutes during business hours.
- After-hours: follow the after-hours rule (below).

## Reviews
- When a job is completed, send the review request.
- If negative feedback is detected, route privately first.

## Weekly report
Every week in `#weekly-report`:
- Total leads
- Booked jobs
- Missed calls
- Avg response time
- Reviews received
- Notes: what we’re changing next week

## After-hours rule (edit per client)
- Option A: immediate text-back + next-day booking follow-up
- Option B: on-call callback window

## Security / boundaries
- Do not paste payment card info.
- Do not share SSNs or sensitive customer data.
- Use threads to keep channels clean.
